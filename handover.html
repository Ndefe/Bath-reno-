Looking after it

Tiles

Wipe them with a soft cloth and warm water. A drop of mild dish soap is enough when they need it. Skip scourers, cream cleanser and acid on the glaze.

Grout

Let the grout dry after a shower. A soft brush and mild soap handles everyday marks. Leave bleach off coloured grout, because it can fade the colour.

Silicone

The bead around the bath, shower and basin is what keeps water out of the wall. Don't pick at it, and don't seal over a split. If it cracks, peels, or stays black after a clean, it needs a new bead.

Tapware

Wipe the tap dry after use, especially where the water is hard. A soft cloth is enough. Skip cream cleanser and steel wool on the finish.
