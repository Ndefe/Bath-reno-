What changes the price

Slab or timber floor

The package from-price keeps the existing layout and does not include cutting a slab.

Relocate toilet (slab cut, new drainage, re-pour) is from $3,200.00 incl. GST, subject to a free site measure. It sits on top of the package.

A timber floor has no catalogue price. The measure looks at the floor.

Moving drains

Moving a drain sits on top of the package.

  • Relocate toilet (slab cut, new drainage, re-pour): from $3,200.00 incl. GST, subject to a free site measure
  • Relocate shower/floor waste: from $2,000.00 incl. GST, subject to a free site measure
  • Relocate basin/vanity plumbing: from $1,000.00 incl. GST, subject to a free site measure

Retile or refresh

The from-price includes new floor tiles and walls to 2.1 m. There is no separate refresh price. A job that keeps the existing tiles is priced after the measure.

Waterproofing

Waterproofing to NCC 2022 Part H4 / Housing Provisions 10.2 (AS 3740:2021) is inside the from-price. A curtain setup is waterproofed across the whole floor. It is not a separate extra.
