Bathroom renovations in Hawthorne

Hawthorne sits between Bulimba and Norman Park, with a small shopping strip and streets of cottages and Queenslanders running toward the river. Many of the older homes have one bathroom that has not been moved since the house was built.

If you want the new bathroom in the same place, the package from-price is the right starting figure. If you want the shower on a different wall, add the relocate extra only after the measure shows the floor can take it. Send nothing yourself. The quote stays on your phone until Nico reviews it.

How a quote works

Start on the quote page. The assistant asks for the room size, a look, a budget, timing and this suburb, then your name and contact. The figure is the package base fee plus its rate per square metre. It is shown as a from-price, incl. GST, subject to a site measure. The lead stays on your phone. Nothing is sent until Nico reviews it.

Waterproofing on these quotes follows NCC 2022 Part H4 / Housing Provisions 10.2 (AS 3740:2021). A curtain shower is waterproofed across the whole floor.

Accessible Care suits self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants and private clients. Gert's Reno is not an NDIS-registered provider. NDIS-funded work needs an OT assessment, and the OT's spec overrides the package. AS 1428.1 is used as a design reference.
