Bathroom renovations in Toowong

Toowong runs from Coronation Drive and the Regatta up toward the foot of Mt Coot-tha. Toowong Village and the High Street are the centre. Near the station there are apartment buildings. The streets behind Sherwood Road and High Street are mostly houses, including Queenslanders and post-war homes.

An apartment bathroom is a different job from a house. The waterproofing has to suit a unit above another home, the hours of noisy work may be limited, and the skip has to have a place to stand. In a house on a sloping block the bathroom is sometimes at the back, with a timber floor on stumps rather than a slab. The quote does not guess which one you have. The measure looks at it.

How a quote works

Start on the quote page. The assistant asks for the room size, a look, a budget, timing and this suburb, then your name and contact. The figure is the package base fee plus its rate per square metre. It is shown as a from-price, incl. GST, subject to a site measure. The lead stays on this device until you copy or send it. Chat text and render prompts go to Pollinations.

Waterproofing on these quotes follows NCC 2022 Part H4 / Housing Provisions 10.2 (AS 3740:2021). A curtain shower is waterproofed across the whole floor.

Accessible Care suits self-managed and plan-managed NDIS participants and private clients. Gert's Reno is not an NDIS-registered provider. NDIS-funded work needs an OT assessment, and the OT's spec overrides the package. AS 1428.1 is used as a design reference.
