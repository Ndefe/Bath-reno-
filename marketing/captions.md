## Week 1 — What a from-price means

A bathroom figure from Gert's Reno is a from-price, incl. GST, subject to a free site measure. It is the package base fee plus a rate per square metre of floor. It is not a fixed quote, and it is not final until Nico confirms it after the measure.

## Week 5 — Waterproofing

Quotes include waterproofing to NCC 2022 Part H4 / Housing Provisions 10.2 (AS 3740:2021). If the shower is a curtain with no screen, the plan is to waterproof the whole floor. The certificate and the exact system are confirmed for the job. This post is not a claim about a particular finished bathroom.

## Week 6 — Accessible Care

Accessible Care is from $30,600 incl. GST at 5 m², subject to a site measure. It is a step-free shower option, grab rails, a fold-down seat and an ambulant-height toilet. Gert's Reno is not an NDIS-registered provider. The package is for self-managed and plan-managed participants, and for private clients. NDIS-funded work needs an OT assessment, and the OT's spec overrides the package. AS 1428.1 is a design reference, not a claim that the room is a compliant bathroom.
